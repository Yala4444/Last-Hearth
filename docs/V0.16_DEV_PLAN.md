# Last Hearth v0.16 — Region Memory, Clean Hub & Character Progression

Status: implemented in v0.16 / regression-tested  
Base: v0.15  
Primary goal: make the world understandable as a persistent place. The player should know what was restored, what remains unknown, why a person comes to the Last Hearth, and what can be built right now.

---

## 1. Product goal

v0.16 is not about adding more content for its own sake. It is a structure-and-meaning pass.

The player should finish a session with four clear answers:
1. Which fire did I restore?
2. What part of the region is now known?
3. Who did I meet, and where did that person go?
4. What can I now build or improve at the Last Hearth?

Core rule:
**One major region = 3 expeditions = 3 restored fires = 1 connected mini-story.**

The region should feel progressively discovered, not fully exposed from the beginning.

---

## 2. Forgotten Forest structure

Forgotten Forest becomes an explicit 3-step chapter.

### Expedition 1 — Edge of the Forest
Purpose:
- teach the core loop;
- restore the first signal fire;
- introduce one meaningful person;
- establish that other fires exist deeper in darkness.

End state:
- forest fire progress = 1/3;
- only Fire 1 is visible on the map;
- the next direction is hinted at, not fully revealed;
- encountered characters get a clear fate.

### Expedition 2 — Old Road / Workshop Belt
Purpose:
- show consequences from Expedition 1;
- introduce a stronger route choice;
- restore Fire 2;
- deepen the relationship with the chapter’s important NPC.

End state:
- progress = 2/3;
- Fire 1 and Fire 2 are visible;
- a vague signal / glow / unknown path toward Fire 3 becomes visible;
- the player understands that the forest is being reclaimed piece by piece.

### Expedition 3 — Heart of the Forest
Purpose:
- conclude the regional story;
- restore Fire 3;
- resolve which character remains locally and which character joins the Last Hearth;
- give a visual and mechanical payoff.

End state:
- progress = 3/3;
- all three discovered fires are visible;
- Forgotten Forest is marked restored/secured;
- the next region is acknowledged but remains hidden/unplayable until implemented;
- one permanent resident may join the Last Hearth.

## 3. Map redesign

Current region map is too high-level. v0.16 adds a discovered-fire layer.

### 3.1 Map hierarchy
The map overlay has two levels:

#### Level A — World / region view
Shows:
- Forgotten Forest;
- future region silhouette only after Forgotten Forest reaches 3/3;
- no fully readable future route before discovery.

#### Level B — Forgotten Forest detail
Shows only discovered information:
- Fire 1 if restored or physically discovered;
- Fire 2 only after reaching / discovering its signal;
- Fire 3 only after reaching / discovering its signal;
- current known path between discovered points;
- unknown darkness beyond the last known point.

### 3.2 Hidden-information rule
Never draw all three future fire nodes from the start.

Use explicit meta state:
- forest_fire_1_state = "unknown" | "seen" | "restored"
- forest_fire_2_state = "unknown" | "seen" | "restored"
- forest_fire_3_state = "unknown" | "seen" | "restored"

On migration from existing v0.15 save:
- forest_fires = 0 -> all unknown;
- 1 -> fire 1 restored, fire 2 seen;
- 2 -> fire 1/2 restored, fire 3 seen;
- 3 -> all restored.

### 3.3 Visual language
Fire states:
- unknown: not drawn at all;
- seen: dim amber glow / vague signal, no full node detail;
- restored: bright fire, small ring, connection path.

The player should be able to open the map and instantly think:
“Here I have already been. Here I saw a signal. Everything beyond that is still darkness.”

### 3.4 Fire detail
Touching a restored fire should show a short card:
- name / location;
- restored status;
- person currently staying there, if any;
- one-line consequence.

Example:
**СЕВЕРНЫЙ ОГОНЬ — ВОССТАНОВЛЕН**  
Охотник остался здесь следить за тропой.  
Путь глубже в лес теперь безопаснее.

No complex sub-screen in v0.16.

## 4. Character progression model

Permanent residents become rare.

### 4.1 Character fate types
Every meaningful NPC must resolve into one of three states:

1. **Temporary companion**
   - helps during the current expedition;
   - does not automatically become a resident.

2. **Local keeper**
   - remains at a restored field fire / outpost;
   - becomes part of that location;
   - may affect future expedition flavor or bonuses.

3. **Last Hearth resident**
   - rare;
   - joins only after a clear narrative reason;
   - unlocks a permanent home and/or new settlement function.

### 4.2 Chapter rule
Target pacing:
**one permanent Last Hearth resident per 3-expedition region.**

This is a pacing target, not an unbreakable future design law, but v0.16 follows it.

### 4.3 Forgotten Forest cast
Recommended v0.16 story resolution:

#### Hunter
- encountered early;
- may accompany the player temporarily;
- after first restoration says the forest route still needs someone watching it;
- remains as keeper of one restored forest fire / outpost;
- does NOT immediately create a house at the Last Hearth.

#### Master / Worker
- appears as the central chapter relationship;
- helps with workshop / repairs during the regional story;
- initially refuses to leave while the forest fires are unstable;
- after 3/3 restored fires, chooses to join the Last Hearth;
- only then does “Дом мастера” become buildable.

#### Forester
For v0.16, do not invent a permanent forester resident just because forest_fires >= 1.
Either:
- keep the role reserved for a later explicit NPC;
or
- convert the current visual/logic into a non-residential supply structure.

Preferred for v0.16:
**remove the automatic forester house entirely until a real forester character exists.**

## 5. Last Hearth availability rules

### 5.1 Initial settlement
Before any permanent resident joins, the hub should show only systems the player actually owns:

- Last Hearth;
- Map table;
- Backpack / storage station;
- Armory / weapon station;
- Hearth upgrade;
- Hearth Book;
- Grove exit;
- Quarry exit.

No empty “house for person X” frame if that person does not live here.

### 5.2 Home unlock rule
A residential construction site becomes visible only when:
- NPC state == "joining_home"
or
- NPC state == "resident".

Example:
- rescued Hunter but Hunter stays at Fire 1 -> no Hunter home in hub;
- Master decides to join after 3/3 -> Master home construction appears.

### 5.3 Build sequence
When a new resident joins:
1. arrival dialogue;
2. short camera/visual focus on empty plot;
3. construction site appears;
4. requirements visible at all times;
5. after completion the resident relocates to the finished house.

## 6. Construction readability

This is P0.

### 6.1 Always-visible requirements
For every active construction site:
- NAME
- WOOD current / required
- STONE current / required

Always visible, not proximity-gated.

Example:

**ДОМ МАСТЕРА**  
ДЕР 2/5    КАМ 1/3

### 6.2 Draw order
Construction information must be rendered after:
- world;
- residents;
- hero;
- normal world props.

Therefore no character can visually cover the requirement card.

### 6.3 Safe layout
Construction labels should not overlap:
- story cards;
- map title;
- route-choice cards;
- residents.

If a large story card is visible, construction cards may dim slightly but remain readable unless the story card physically occupies their zone.

### 6.4 Upgrade stations
Backpack / Armory / Hearth use fixed positions near the central fire and clearly show:
- current effect;
- next effect;
- ember cost.

Example:
**РЮКЗАК**  
Слоты 6  
след.: 7 слотов • 5 углей

**ОРУЖЕЙНАЯ**  
Урон +10%  
след.: +20% • 6 углей

## 7. Choice UI redesign

Current left/right workshop choice can visually collide with world content.

### 7.1 Fixed choice anchors
All binary progression choices use two fixed safe anchors around the hearth:
- left card center around x=120;
- right card center around x=360;
- y chosen from one safe shared band.

No choice label may be tied directly to a moving character.

### 7.2 Choice card anatomy
Each side gets:
- title;
- exact effect;
- one short flavor phrase;
- visual icon/silhouette.

Example:

**ОРУЖИЕ**  
УРОН +28%  
“Быстрее заканчивать бой”

**ПОХОДНЫЙ НАБОР**  
СЛОТ +1 • ЗДР +18  
“Дольше идти и больше нести”

### 7.3 Confirmation feedback
After selection:
- hide both choice cards immediately;
- show one clean consequence card;
- update top stat strip;
- do not leave the unchosen card visible behind the message.

---

## 8. Notification system rules

v0.15 queue stays, but v0.16 formalizes screen ownership.

### 8.1 Priority
1. critical story / NPC dialogue;
2. major state change;
3. choice consequence;
4. construction completion;
5. small pickup/combat feedback.

### 8.2 One owner per screen zone
Top narrative lane:
- only one large card.

Center interaction lane:
- choice UI OR construction focus OR route prompt.

World labels:
- only short object names.

### 8.3 Character avoidance
Characters do not need AI collision with UI, but critical UI must be drawn above them with opaque background.

For static hub residents:
- idle positions must never sit directly under active construction cards.

### 8.4 Duration
Story card: ~3.2–4.8 s depending on length.  
Major consequence: ~2.2–3.0 s.  
Minor feedback: ~1.1–1.6 s.

No repeated card should reset another card indefinitely.

## 9. Hearth Book redesign

The Hearth Book becomes an atmospheric codex, not a wall of tutorial text.

### 9.1 Visual format
Two-page feeling inside one mobile panel:
- dark paper / book frame;
- gold section headers;
- small fire glyphs;
- shorter paragraphs;
- section navigation by tapping bottom tabs/arrows.

No need for real page-turn animation in v0.16.

### 9.2 Page structure

#### Page 1 — “ПОСЛЕДНИЙ ОЧАГ”
Text purpose:
Explain the fantasy.

Suggested copy:
“Когда старые огни погасли, дороги исчезли вместе с ними.  
Последний Очаг — место, куда ещё возвращаются люди.  
Каждый восстановленный огонь отвоёвывает у тьмы ещё один кусок мира.”

#### Page 2 — “ВЫЛАЗКИ”
- light the field fire;
- carry fuel;
- choose routes;
- survive the night;
- restore the regional signal.

#### Page 3 — “ЛЮДИ”
Explain:
- not every rescued person comes home;
- some remain at restored fires;
- some help temporarily;
- a few may choose the Last Hearth.

#### Page 4 — “ПОСЕЛЕНИЕ”
- wood/stone = physical construction;
- embers = permanent upgrades;
- homes appear when someone actually needs one.

#### Page 5 — “КАРТА”
- only discovered fires are shown;
- dim signal = seen, not restored;
- bright signal = restored;
- darkness means unknown, not locked content.

### 9.3 Contextual unlock
Pages can reveal gradually:
- Map page after first restored fire;
- People page after first meaningful NPC;
- Settlement page on first return home.

## 10. Story spine for Forgotten Forest

v0.16 should make the three expeditions feel connected.

### 10.1 Expedition 1
Theme: “Someone is still alive out there.”

Key beat:
- restore first field signal;
- meet Hunter or equivalent;
- Hunter helps;
- after the night, Hunter says someone must stay and watch the route;
- Hunter becomes local keeper, not a home resident.

Map payoff:
Fire 1 appears restored.  
Fire 2 becomes only a vague signal.

### 10.2 Expedition 2
Theme: “The forest once had infrastructure.”

Key beat:
- follow the second signal;
- find ruined workshop / Master;
- clear access;
- repair something together;
- Master says the workshop/fires are not secure enough to leave yet.

Map payoff:
Fire 2 restored.  
Fire 3 becomes a vague signal.

### 10.3 Expedition 3
Theme: “Restore the chain.”

Key beat:
- deepest route;
- stronger pressure;
- final signal fire;
- surviving local NPCs can now maintain the network;
- Master decides to come to the Last Hearth.

Example dialogue:
“Теперь огни видят друг друга.  
Охотник удержит тропу, остальные справятся здесь.  
А у твоего Очагa всё ещё слишком много работы для одного человека.  
Я пойду с тобой.”

Then:
- permanent resident flag set;
- Master home site unlocks.

## 11. Regional persistence

Restored fires should matter after the run.

Meta should store:
- fire state;
- local keeper;
- short outcome id;
- chapter completion state.

Suggested:
forest_fire_1_state
forest_fire_2_state
forest_fire_3_state
forest_fire_1_keeper
forest_fire_2_keeper
forest_fire_3_keeper
forest_story_step
master_relationship_state

No need for free-roam revisiting every fire in v0.16.  
The map and later story references are enough for this version.

---

## 12. Settlement growth after each fire

### After 1/3
- central fire / light slightly stronger;
- supply pile appears;
- map gains first restored node;
- no fake houses.

### After 2/3
- extra path lantern;
- defensive stakes / crates;
- settlement starts to look prepared;
- still no residential plot unless someone actually committed to joining.

### After 3/3
- stronger main fire;
- perimeter lights;
- “Forgotten Forest restored” state;
- Master arrives;
- Master home construction unlocks;
- next region silhouette appears on map, still hidden/unplayable.

---

## 13. What v0.16 deliberately does NOT include

To keep scope controlled:

- no full Dead Fields chapter yet;
- no huge NPC dialogue system;
- no relationship meters;
- no procedural open-world map;
- no dozens of named residents;
- no inventory RPG screen;
- no town-management simulation.

v0.16 is the vertical slice proving:
**discover -> restore -> remember -> person chooses a fate -> home changes.**

## 15. Save migration

Existing v0.15 users must not lose progress.

Migration:
- infer discovered/restored fires from meta.forest_fires;
- preserve embers, carry_level, damage_level, hearth_bonus;
- old rescued_hunter should NOT automatically imply hub resident;
- old rescued_worker:
  - if forest_fires >= 3, convert to master resident/joining state;
  - otherwise convert to chapter NPC state, not home resident;
- preserve already-spent permanent upgrade resources;
- deprecated forester/hunter home progress can remain stored but hidden until a future valid resident uses it, or be migrated to generic settlement materials if needed.

Preferred safe behavior:
do not delete old keys; ignore deprecated residential sites until valid.

## 17. Acceptance criteria

v0.16 is complete when a new player can answer without guessing:

- “I restored the first of three forest fires.”
- “I can see that fire on the map.”
- “I cannot see undiscovered future fires.”
- “That Hunter stayed there; he did not magically move into my settlement.”
- “The Master has not joined me yet because the forest chapter is unfinished.”
- “There are no empty houses for residents I do not have.”
- “I can always see exactly how much wood/stone a real construction needs.”
- “I understand what each choice gives before I choose it.”
- “The Hearth Book explains the world, not just the controls.”
- “After 3/3, the Forgotten Forest feels completed and the Last Hearth visibly changes.”

## 18. Design principle locked for future versions

**The Last Hearth grows slowly. A permanent resident is a character arc, not a level reward.**

**The map reveals knowledge, not future content.**

**Every restored fire remains part of the world after the run ends.**

**Every new settlement object must have a reason to exist.**
