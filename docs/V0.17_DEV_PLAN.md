# Last Hearth v0.17 — The World Takes Shape

Status: implementation pass
Base: v0.16 Region Memory & Real Residents

## Goal

v0.17 should make Last Hearth feel like the beginning of a whole game rather than one loop repeated forever.

The core loop remains recognizable:

light -> explore -> make a meaningful choice -> prepare -> survive -> restore a regional fire -> return home.

But every region must change one fundamental rule of play, every 3-fire chapter must have its own escalation, and permanent residents must be earned through character arcs.

## Global chapter spine

### Chapter I — Forgotten Forest
Core rule: light reveals a living, branching wilderness.
Permanent resident: Master.
Supporting fates: Hunter can become a local keeper; Scouts/guards can remain temporary.
Chapter escalation:
- Fire 1 — Forest Edge. Teaches the loop. Final night ends with BLACK BOAR, a charge-focused mini-boss.
- Fire 2 — Old Road. Infrastructure, ruins and tighter decisions. Final night ends with ROOTBORN, a slow area-pressure mini-boss.
- Fire 3 — Heart of the Forest. Faster setup, accumulated pressure, chapter payoff. Final night ends with FOREST GUARDIAN, the true multi-phase regional boss.

### Chapter II — Dead Fields
Core rule: exposed ground, sparse cover and danger outside protected lanes.
Permanent resident: Healer.
Settlement unlock: Infirmary / between-expedition recovery.
Other people can become fire keepers, travelers or temporary fighters.

### Chapter III — Flooded Lowlands
Core rule: water changes movement and routes; crossings must be opened or repaired.
Permanent resident: Cartographer / Pathfinder.
Settlement unlock: map intelligence; limited advance information about possible routes.

### Chapter IV — Ashen Ridge
Core rule: wind destabilizes fire; heat, height and mineral deposits compete for attention.
Permanent resident: Smith.
Settlement unlock: weapon families / meaningful weapon modifications rather than flat damage only.

### Chapter V — Old City
Core rule: several objectives can be active at once; the player cannot save everything.
Permanent resident: Archivist / Keeper.
Settlement unlock: late-game world history and advanced permanent systems.
This chapter should resolve what happened to the old fire network and set up the endgame.

## Region rule

Every region = 3 fires, but not 3 copies of the same run.

Each region must change at least:
1. one resource/setup rule;
2. one exploration rule;
3. one night-defense rule;
4. one chapter boss pattern;
5. one permanent settlement capability.

Do not create a new biome whose only difference is art.

## Forgotten Forest v0.17 pass

### Fire 1 — Edge
Tone: someone is still alive out there.
Keep the clearest onboarding version of the current loop.
Route pool: Hunter vs Sawmill.
Middle: Master encounter.
Final preparation: Watch vs Fire Mark.
Final enemy: BLACK BOAR.
Boss identity: bursts of speed/charges; readable pauses between bursts.
Reward: first restored fire; Hunter may remain its keeper.

### Fire 2 — Old Road
Tone: the forest once had infrastructure.
Route pool: Caravan vs Nest.
Middle: Storehouse.
Final preparation: Barricade vs Stone Circle.
Final enemy: ROOTBORN.
Boss identity: slow, durable, periodically hardens and pressures the hearth with roots.
Reward: second restored fire; Master remains temporarily to stabilize this link.

### Fire 3 — Heart
Tone: close the chain.
Route pool: Signal Scout vs Dead Beacon.
Middle: Scout.
Final preparation: Beacon vs Guard Cache.
Final enemy: FOREST GUARDIAN only here.
Boss identity:
- phase 1: measured approach;
- phase 2 at ~66% HP: becomes faster and calls lesser creatures;
- phase 3 at ~33% HP: strongest pressure, darker presentation and final push toward the hearth.
Reward: three fires see each other; Forgotten Forest complete; Master chooses Last Hearth.

## Replay policy

Story chapters are not reset after completion.
A restored region stays restored.

No free replay/patrol mode in v0.17.

Possible later system: Patrols in restored regions for rare threats, travelers or resources. This must never pretend the regional fires were extinguished again.

## Resident rule

Target: roughly one major permanent resident per 3-fire chapter.

A chapter may contain many people, but most should have another fate:
- temporary companion;
- local keeper;
- traveler;
- casualty / unresolved person;
- later visitor.

Permanent resident must:
1. have appeared before joining;
2. have a reason to leave the region;
3. unlock a concrete settlement function;
4. visually change Last Hearth;
5. require or justify a home/workplace.

## Sound pass

v0.17 introduces a first procedural sound layer so the build is no longer silent.

Required:
- fire ambience;
- forest/wind ambience;
- axe / stone gathering impact;
- pickup;
- player shot;
- enemy death;
- night transition;
- mini-boss / boss arrival;
- map discovery / restored-fire acknowledgement;
- return-home accent.

Sound must support readability. Silence before the true Forest Guardian is part of the design.

## Visual polish

Do not redesign the whole art direction.

Improve the current stylized prototype with:
- stronger contact shadows;
- warmer windows / local light around completed homes;
- role-readable NPC accessories;
- small lived-in props around resident buildings;
- clearer boss silhouettes;
- stronger final-boss visual phases;
- slightly richer Last Hearth growth at 1/3, 2/3 and 3/3.

## Architecture to prepare now

Represent future content as region profiles, not scattered hard-coded assumptions.

Each region profile should define:
- id;
- display name;
- three fire names;
- core gameplay modifier;
- chapter resident;
- settlement unlock;
- final boss id.

Only Forgotten Forest is playable in v0.17.
Future regions remain design data until their chapter implementation begins.

## Acceptance criteria

v0.17 is successful when:
- Fire 1, Fire 2 and Fire 3 no longer end with the same boss;
- Forest Guardian appears only in Fire 3;
- the three forest finales feel mechanically different;
- the game has audible feedback and ambience;
- the Last Hearth and residents look slightly more alive without a full redesign;
- the global five-region progression is documented and represented in code;
- restored chapters remain permanent;
- all existing v0.16 progression and save behavior still passes regression tests.
